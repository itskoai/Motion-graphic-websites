# DIRECTION · דרך האות (Through the Letter)

The one idea every scene builder follows. Read it fully before touching a scene. Chosen by a three-judge panel over two alternatives; their warnings are folded in below as rules.

## 1. Premise
The site is a sheet of warm paper and ink type. Footage never appears in a rectangle: it appears only INSIDE Hebrew letters, as if each headline were cut out of the paper and the studio's films ran behind it. One letter per headline is the window (always a letter with a roof: ם ה ת ח, and ד only at very large sizes). Scrolling zooms into the roof of that letter (the thick top stroke) until the stroke is the whole screen and the type has become the image; scrolling back pulls the film back into the word. Every cut on the site happens inside a letter. Weight and width of a single variable Hebrew serif are the only animation vocabulary: words thicken to take attention, thin to give it away, condense to make room, widen to breathe. No glow, no gradients, no cards. A visitor sees Hebrew letters do things they have not seen letters do, and the films do the proof work.

The page is paper from start to end, with ONE inversion to ink (scene 7, the anticipation) and back. Full-bleed footage moments (hero, signature, gallery open state) are the only times the paper is covered.

## 2. Tokens (globals.css `@theme`)
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-paper` | `#F1EADC` | page background, the warm sheet (everything except scene 7) |
| `--color-ink` | `#16140F` | all type, hairlines, and the background of scene 7 |
| `--color-graphite` | `#5B574E` | secondary text, captions, placeholder labels (6.4:1 on paper) |
| `--color-ash` | `#CFC8B8` | retired words (weight 200), baseline rails, placeholder fills; decorative only, never information |
| `--color-window` | `#0A0A0A` | the backing behind every media window: what shows before a video is ready |
| `--color-bone` | `#ECE6DA` | type on ink (scene 7 only) |
| `--color-mark` | `#1F4FD1` | ONE notebook-ballpoint blue: the CTA underline, the focus ring, the single live link per scene, the word קונספט (5.4:1 on paper). On ink use `--color-mark-on-ink #7D97FF`. |
Rules: no other colours. Media inside windows is graded so the letter still reads as a letter (brightness ≤ 0.7 at rest, with a 1px ink stroke along the glyph edge); at full-bleed the grade is lifted. No gradients, no shadows, no blur, no cards with backgrounds. Frames are letters; the only rectangles on the site are the drawn storyboard panels and the drawn browser chrome in scene 3 and the drawn portrait frames in scene 5.

Materials: `html[data-material]` is `paper` (default) or `ink` (scene 7). `useSceneProgress({material})` sets it. A scene that crosses the inversion uses the `.material.m-paper-ink` blend with its own `--p`.

## 3. Typography
- Display: **Noto Serif Hebrew variable** (self-hosted, axes `wght 100..900`, `wdth 62.5..100`, `font-display: block`), `var(--font-display)`. The display instance for window letters is fixed: **900 / 62.5** (`.hl` sets `font-variation-settings: "wght" var(--w, 900), "wdth" var(--wd, 62.5)`). Headlines are SSR real text (LCP, SEO, screen readers); the window letter is a `<span class="win">` inside the same text (see §5).
- Axis animation budget (hard rule): only ONE pinned headline per scene animates `--w`/`--wd`, ten glyphs or fewer, snapped to 30 steps, in a fixed-height box with `will-change: font-variation-settings`. Index rows change weight by class with a CSS transition on hover/in-view, never per scroll frame. Body text never animates axes. On phones animate `wght` only.
- Body: **IBM Plex Sans Hebrew 300/400/500**, 18px/1.6 desktop, 17px/1.55 phone. Mono: **IBM Plex Mono 400**, Latin only, for formats and counts: `9:16`, `24 FPS`, `32 FR`, `1120×700`, `SC 03`. Hebrew never goes in mono.
- Tracking on Hebrew capped at 0.08em. No nikud. `unicode-bidi: isolate` on split spans. Trailing periods must land on the visual left (verify in screenshots).
- Sizes: hero 11–14vw desktop, phone one word per line at ≥ 30vw so the window letter is ≥ 110px wide. `.hl-xl`, `.hl-l`, `.hl-m` in globals.

## 4. The window primitive (built, in `components/window/`)
`LetterWindow` renders a headline as real text where one letter (the window) is transparent, and a `<canvas>` draws media clipped to that letter's exact outline (glyph paths pre-extracted from the font at 900/62.5 in `lib/glyphs.ts`, so the window never depends on font loading and works in Safari; no SVG clip-path on video anywhere). Props: `text` (with `[ם]` marking the window letter), `media` (a video source pair or a frame sequence), `zoom` (0 = resting inside the letter, 1 = the roof fills the viewport: full-bleed), `grade`. The zoom is a canvas transform about the roof's centre; above the point where the stroke covers the screen the clip is dropped (cheap full-bleed draw) and restored on reverse. Use `useLetterWindow` for scroll-driven zoom (it takes the scene's `--p` segment). Media lifecycle: the video or frames decode only while the scene is near the viewport; posters everywhere else; 40px hysteresis on play/pause.

## 5. Motion vocabulary (the only moves allowed)
1. Weight/width trade: the active word 900/62.5 in ink, the retired word 200/100 in ash. No opacity fades between words.
2. Roof zoom: the window letter's roof scales until full-bleed (canvas transform), reversible.
3. Media inside letters: a loop plays at rest; a frame sequence scrubs with `--p` during the zoom.
4. Draw: SVG `stroke-dashoffset` driven by `--p` (storyboard panels, pencil silhouettes, the process diagrams, the ballpoint rule under the CTA).
5. Baseline rail: words slide along a shared baseline (translateX only).
Transforms, opacity on type only, filters on media only. Nothing runs on a timer except the hero's 1.2s entrance and the slit loop inside letters. Native scroll always; no smooth-scroll library; pins release cleanly at both ends.

## 6. The scenes (ids fixed; the nav links to them)
1. `#top` **פתיחה**. Paper. Wordmark small (Plex 500, config name). Right-aligned, two lines at 11–14vw desktop: "אתרים, סרטים וסיפורים" / "שעוד לא ראיתם." The **ם of ראיתם** is the window. Under it Plex 300: the positioning line; CTA as text "בואו ניצור משהו יחד" with a `--mark` ballpoint rule that draws itself under it; beside it "לצפות בריל" opens the real showreel in the Viewer (chain: opening film → series reel → drill clip; see content). Phones: "שעוד לא" / "ראיתם." on separate lines, ראיתם at ≥ 30vw. On load (1.2s, no scroll): glyphs arrive right to left tweening wght 100→900 and wdth 100→62.5 with 40ms stagger; the window opens when the tween ends and the 4s light-slit loop (`/media/door-loop.webm|mp4`) plays inside the ם at brightness 0.7. Pin 300vh desktop / 200vh phone, scrub: p 0..0.12 hold; 0.12..0.8 roof zoom to full-bleed, the media swapping at zoom ≈ 0.4 to the opening film scrubbed by p (desktop /media/hero/001..134.webp 1280×720; phones /media/herom/00..67.webp 720×1280); 0.8..1 hold full-bleed: a small ink play affordance "▶ הריל" at the bottom start corner opens the chained showreel in the Viewer. Full-bleed footage must be reached within ~600px of scroll on desktop. Before the font and first frame are ready the window shows the poster still of the slit (`/media/door-poster.jpg`), never a blank rectangle.
2. `#create` **שלוש מילים**. Three words stacked on the right at ~16vw, one shared baseline rail in ash: "אתרים" / "סרטים" / "סיפורים" (all end in final mem: the ם is the window of each). Left of each one Plex 300 line, max 9 words: "אתרים שזזים כמו סרט." / "סרטים שנושמים." / "סיפורים שנשארים בראש." Pin 400vh desktop / 240vh phone, three thirds. Handover is a pure weight/width trade. The caption slides along the rail to the active word. Windows have a minimum rendered height of 120px on phones.
3. `#story` **מרעיון לחוויה** (the signature). One pinned stage (500vh desktop / 320vh phone), five words appear in sequence on a single baseline at ~14vw: "רעיון", "סקיצה", "תמונה", "סצנה", "חוויה" with a mono counter 01/05..05/05 at the start edge and one Plex 300 caption per state (content story.steps). רעיון: wght 100, a pencil-thin word on paper, the idea line. סקיצה: the letters switch to outline (1px ink stroke, no fill) and four thin rectangles built from the letters' bounding boxes draw themselves (stroke-dashoffset) as storyboard panels with mono SC 01..04; each panel fills with umbrella frame d00 under `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.7)` and `mix-blend-mode: multiply` (the pencil version of the real still). תמונה: the **ה** becomes the window with umbrella frame d00 as a still; outlines fill back to 900. סצנה: roof zoom of the ה; frames d00..d31 scrub inside until full-bleed (the umbrella figure moves). חוויה: pull back; the window now holds the merch capture inside a drawn browser chrome (SVG, Hebrew nav, mono DEMO) and the word sits in ink beneath: a finished digital experience. 4. `#work` **עבודות נבחרות**. An editorial index, not a grid: rows at ~7vw in 500/100, a mono number column at the start (01..07) and a Plex 300 tag column at the end. Rows (content work.items): 01 דור העתיד (series reel + night-street look-book stills as the hover preview), 02 הקיר (drill clip), 03 אתר המרצ׳ (merch capture), 04 קו של אור (opening film), 05 המטרייה · קונספט, 06 דמויות · חומרי הפקה (face sheet stepping through its six cells, the 360 turnaround spun by scroll), 07 עבודות לקוחות · בקרוב (an honest row, weight 100 outline, never opens). Keyboard: focus opens the preview, Enter zooms, Escape closes.

7. `#next` **את זה עוד לא פיתחנו.** The ONE inversion: the sheet goes to ink (`material: 'ink'`, bone type), scrubbed by the scene's own `--p` so it cannot desync. The line "את זה עוד לא פיתחנו." at ~12vw, the **ח of פיתחנו** is the window holding the light-slit still (`/media/door-poster.jpg`) that develops across the scroll (brightness 0.35 → 0.6: the only motion in the scene). Tracking ≤ 0.08em. Then two Plex lines and the small notify form (email + the exact consent line). The form's link to the contact form preselects the topic. No dates, no product words. 8. `#contact` **בואו ניצור משהו יחד.** Paper. ## 7. Phones (390px) and reduced motion
- Pin budget: hero 200vh, create 240vh, story 320vh; nothing else pinned. Total desktop pins 300+400+500 = 1200vh.
- Phones: window letters on their own line at ≥ 30vw; windows ≥ 120px tall; portrait frame sets (`herom`, `idea/m*`); the hero may start partially zoomed (scale ≈ 3 inside the roof) so moving footage is visible in the first viewport. Frame sequences are drawn at viewport size, decoded lazily around the current index (never all 68 at full size).
- Reduced motion (`useMotionMode().reduced`) or `saveData`: no pins, no scrubs, no entrance tweens, no axis animation (weights static 900/62.5); every window holds its poster still; the positioning line, CTA and contact form render without any ScrollTrigger; the scene 6 diagrams complete; material switches without transition.
- 44px touch targets, 16px gutters, no horizontal scroll (html overflow-x: clip). Landscape phones: treat as reduced.

## 8. Honesty
Founders: no names, roles, portraits, history; drawn silhouettes with designed labels. Work: only the studio's own pieces; קונספט and חומרי הפקה labels in `--mark`; row 07 is openly "עבודות לקוחות · בקרוב". No testimonials, no logos, no numbers, no prices, no dates. The future offering is one inverted scene with one line and a form. Yuval and Itay are characters the studio made, never real children, never founders; say so once in the gallery.
